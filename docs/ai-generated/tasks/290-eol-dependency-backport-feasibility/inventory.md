### Why the Numbers Shifted Since August 2026

1. **Tier 1 Java 8 upgrades already landed on `main`:** Jackson (`2.21.7`), Netty (`4.1.137.Final`), Log4j (`2.26.1`), Tomcat (`9.0.121`), POI (`5.4.0`), SnakeYAML (`2.6`), Jsoup (`1.23.2`), Junrar (`7.6.1`), Snappy (`1.1.10.9`), and Protobuf (`4.x`) closed hundreds of August 2026 matches.
2. **Legacy replacements removed several GAVs:** `jasypt`, `jettison`, `json-lib`, and `rhino` are gone from the live SBOM.
3. **NVD CPE false-positives vs. exact Maven coordinate matching:** `owasp-suppressions.xml` carried historical CVEs already fixed in shipped versions (e.g., `CVE-2024-47072` fixed in `xstream:1.4.21`, `CVE-2025-48976` fixed in `commons-fileupload:1.6.0`, `CVE-2024-47554` fixed in `commons-io:2.21.0`, `CVE-2025-48734` fixed in `commons-beanutils:1.11.0`, `CVE-2024-38827` fixed in `spring-security-core:5.8.16`, `CVE-2024-38829` fixed in `spring-ldap-core:2.4.4`).
4. **23 of the 84 open Dependabot alerts (27.4%) do NOT require a new EOL fork:** They are direct Java 8 bumps (`guava`, `zookeeper`, `jakarta.el`), transitive version mismatches against the existing Jetty `9.4.65.1` fork or `bc*-jdk15to18:1.85`, or dead transitive artifacts (`shiro`).
